**Mathematical Resilience Threshold:**
- **Resilience Function:** Let \( R(t) \) represent the resilience function at time \( t \). The resilience function measures the network's ability to maintain operations under adverse conditions:
  \[
  R(t) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{availability}(i, t)
  \]
  where \( N \) is the number of nodes in the network, and \( \text{availability}(i, t) \) is the availability of node \( i \) at time \( t \).

  R_c = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{availability}_c(i)
