\[ O = f(D) \]

\[ k = \frac{2E}{V} \]

\[ B(v) = \sum_{s \neq v \neq t} \frac{\sigma_{st}(v)}{\sigma_{st}} \]

where \( \sigma_{st} \) is the total number of shortest paths from node \( s \) to node \( t \), and \( \sigma_{st}(v) \) is the number of those paths passing through node \( v \).

\[ g(P, U) = \begin{cases}

\end{cases} \]

\[ MU = \frac{dU}{dQ} \]
\[ MC = \frac{dP}{dQ} \]
